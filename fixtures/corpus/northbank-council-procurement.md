# Northbank City Council: Procurement Guide (fixture)

This is an original fixture document written for testing Keel. It describes a fictional council.

## Purpose

This guide sets out how Northbank City Council staff buy goods and services. It applies to every
purchase made with council funds, from a box of pens to a multi-year contract.

## Thresholds

Purchases are grouped into four bands by total value including GST:

- Band 1: up to $5,000. One verbal or written quote is sufficient. The buyer records the supplier and
  price in the purchasing register.
- Band 2: $5,001 to $50,000. Three written quotes are required. The buyer attaches all three quotes to
  the purchase order.
- Band 3: $50,001 to $250,000. A public request for quotation is required, open for at least ten
  business days on the council's tender portal.
- Band 4: above $250,000. A public tender is required, open for at least twenty-one business days, and
  the evaluation panel must have three members including one from Finance.

## Approvals

Band 1 and Band 2 purchases are approved by the team leader. Band 3 purchases are approved by the
director of the relevant division. Band 4 purchases are approved by the Chief Executive Officer after a
recommendation from the evaluation panel.

## Conflicts of interest

Every panel member signs a conflict of interest declaration before evaluation begins. A panel member who
declares a conflict steps down and is replaced. Declarations are kept for seven years.

## Local supplier weighting

Evaluations for Band 3 and Band 4 purchases apply a ten percent weighting in favour of suppliers whose
principal place of business is inside the Northbank local government area.

## Records

Purchase records are kept for seven years after the final payment. Contracts above Band 3 are published
on the council's contracts register within thirty days of signing.

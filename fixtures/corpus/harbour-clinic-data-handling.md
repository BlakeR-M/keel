# Harbour Clinic: Data Handling Standard (fixture)

Original fixture document for testing Keel. Harbour Clinic is fictional.

## Scope

This standard applies to all staff and contractors of Harbour Clinic who create, access, store or send
patient information.

## Classification

Information is classified into three levels:

- Public: information already published by the clinic, such as opening hours.
- Internal: business information not intended for publication, such as rosters and supplier lists.
- Restricted: any information that identifies a patient or relates to a patient's care.

## Storage

Restricted information is stored only in the practice management system or the clinical document store.
It is never stored on personal devices, in personal email, or in consumer cloud storage. Internal
information may be stored on clinic-managed devices and the clinic's shared drive.

## Sending

Restricted information is sent to another provider only through the secure messaging gateway. Email is
used for restricted information only when the recipient's address has been verified by phone and the
message is encrypted. Fax is not used.

## Retention

Adult patient records are retained for seven years after the last entry. Records for patients who were
under eighteen at the last entry are retained until the patient turns twenty-five.

## Access

Access to restricted information is granted by role. Reception staff see demographic and appointment
data. Clinicians see full records for patients under their care. The practice manager approves any
access outside these roles and records the reason.

## Breaches

A suspected breach is reported to the practice manager within one hour of discovery. The practice manager
assesses whether the breach is likely to cause serious harm and, if so, notifies affected patients and the
regulator within thirty days.
